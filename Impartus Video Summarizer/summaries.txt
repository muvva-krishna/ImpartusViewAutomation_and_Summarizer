# Digital Design: SR and D Latches

## SR Latch
- **Definition**: A basic memory storage element that can hold one bit of information.
- **Components**: Constructed using NOR or NAND gates.
- **Functionality**: 
  - Set (S) and Reset (R) inputs control the output state.
  - When S=1 and R=0, output Q is set to 1.
  - When S=0 and R=1, output Q is reset to 0.
  - When S=0 and R=0, output maintains its previous state.
  - Invalid state occurs when both S and R are 1 (for NOR) or 0 (for NAND).

- **Definition**: A variation of the SR latch that eliminates the invalid state.

Summary: # PN-Junction at Equilibrium

## Overview of PN-Junctions
- A PN-junction is formed by joining p-type and n-type semiconductors.
- It plays a crucial role in semiconductor devices.

## Equilibrium Condition
- At equilibrium, the diffusion of charge carriers (electrons and holes) is balanced by the drift due to the electric field.
- The Fermi level is constant throughout the junction.

## Key Concepts
- **Charge Carrier Concentration**: The concentration of holes in the p-type region and electrons in the n-type region.
- **Depletion Region**: A region around the junction where mobile charge carriers are depleted, leading to an electric field.

## Mathematical Relations
- The area under the concentration vs. position (x) plot can be used to derive important parameters.
- Relations involving the equilibrium quantities can be invoked to analyze the behavior of the junction.

## Importance of Equilibrium Analysis
- Understanding the equilibrium state is essential for predicting the behavior
